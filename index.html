TIM Global Navigation

User Types

Anonymous Visitor

Splash Landing, Topic Center, Article Show — full logotype nav with a Join CTA, no account.

Subscriber

Same top nav and landing as the Visitor, with a "Finish up now" upsell in place of Join.

Logged In Member

Home hub, profile dropdown, and every uplevel content screen — articles, groups, programs, profiles, Q&A, activity.

Logged Out Member

Gated home hub for someone with an account who isn’t currently signed in.

Entry Points

Paid Social – Article

A Facebook ad opens straight into an Article Show.

Organic + Paid Search – Article

A Google search result opens straight into an Article Show.

Organic + Paid Search – Home

A Google search result opens on the Splash Landing home.

Content Email – Article

A Gmail message opens Logged In Member straight into an Article Show.
